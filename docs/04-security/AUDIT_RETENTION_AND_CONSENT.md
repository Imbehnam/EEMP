# Audit, Retention, Consent, and Data Rights

## Audit events
Capture actor, tenant, scope, action, resource type/id, timestamp, correlation ID, result, reason, and relevant before/after metadata with sensitive fields redacted. Separate security audit from business activity history where needed.

## High-value audit events
Role/permission changes; support access grant/use; student/guardian record access where warranted; grade edits/approval/publication; invoice issue/refund; payment state; workflow transitions; data referral/export; consent changes; tenant configuration; subscription changes.

## Consent and authority
Record subject, guardian/representative, scope, purpose, legal basis, timestamp, collection method, expiry, withdrawal, and evidence. Legal requirements vary by jurisdiction and age.

## Retention
Define retention by data category and purpose. Support legal hold, export, correction, anonymization, and deletion where lawful. Audit/financial retention may differ from academic/counseling data retention.
